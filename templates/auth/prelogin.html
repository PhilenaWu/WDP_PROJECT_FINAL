Frequently Asked

Questions we hear often

Is Genlink free to use?
Yes — Genlink is completely free for all members. Creating an account, joining events, messaging connections, and participating in the community never costs anything.
Who can join Genlink?
Genlink is open to anyone aged 13 and above living in Singapore. We welcome youth, working adults, and seniors alike. Our community spans all ethnicities, languages, and backgrounds — the more diverse, the richer the connections.
Is the platform safe for seniors who are new to technology?
Absolutely. Genlink is designed with accessibility in mind — large text, clear navigation, and simple steps. All messaging is private and moderated. We also run digital literacy workshops so seniors can get comfortable using the platform with support from our volunteer network.
Can I attend events if I am not a registered member?
Some community events are open to the public, but RSVP-ing, getting reminders, and connecting with other attendees all require a free Genlink account. Sign-up takes under two minutes — we'd love for you to join!
How is my personal information protected?
Your privacy is a priority. We comply with Singapore's PDPA (Personal Data Protection Act). Your personal details are never sold to third parties. You control what appears on your public profile, and you can delete your account and data at any time.
